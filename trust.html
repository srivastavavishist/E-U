Methodology & Data Sources

Energy Intelligence is an independent, public-source research reader for the global Energy & Utilities sector. This page explains how data is gathered, summarised, and presented.

Sources

All content is derived exclusively from publicly available information, including:

  • Regulatory filings: 10-K, 10-Q, 20-F (SEC EDGAR), annual reports
  • Official company press releases and investor relations pages
  • Regulatory body publications (FERC, NRC, EPA, OFGEM, AEMO)
  • Public earnings call transcripts and presentations
  • International Energy Agency (IEA) and government energy statistics
  • News wire services (Reuters, Bloomberg news, S&P Global Commodity Insights)

Independence

This reader is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any energy company, financial institution, or government body. No compensation is received from any company featured in this publication.

Summaries and sentiment labels reflect the editorial interpretation of publicly available facts. They are not investment advice and should not be relied upon for financial decisions.

Accuracy & Updates

Data is updated on a rolling basis as new public information becomes available. Market capitalisation figures are approximate and based on publicly available sources at the time of publication. Historical data may not be updated retroactively.

Privacy

This site does not collect personal data, set tracking cookies, or use third-party analytics. No account creation or login is required. All data is processed client-side in your browser.

Limitations

  • Coverage is selective — not all energy companies are included
  • Private companies may have estimated or unavailable financial data
  • This reader is not a substitute for primary source due diligence
  • Sector tags are editorial classifications and may differ from official SIC/NAICS codes
